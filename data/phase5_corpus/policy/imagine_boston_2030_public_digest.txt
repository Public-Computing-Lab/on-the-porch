Title: Imagine Boston 2030 — public planning themes (digest)
Publisher: City of Boston
Official URL: https://www.boston.gov/departments/planning-development
Geography: Boston / Dorchester
Source type: PLANNING_POLICY

Imagine Boston 2030 is the city's long-range plan describing goals for housing, mobility,
open space, and economic opportunity. Planning documents guide city investments and
priorities; specific zoning changes and capital projects still require separate formal
actions. Residents should consult the official plan materials for complete language.
