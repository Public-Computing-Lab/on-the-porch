Title: Codman Square Neighborhood Development Corporation — public overview
Publisher: Codman Square NDC (community organization)
Official URL: https://www.codmansquare.org/
Geography: Codman Square / Dorchester
Source type: COMMUNITY_OPINION

Codman Square NDC is a neighborhood-based community development organization serving
residents around Codman Square with housing, economic development, and community programs.
Community organization materials describe local priorities and services; they are not City
of Boston ordinances.
