Title: Where to find official Boston zoning information
Publisher: On the Porch curated reference
Provenance: OFFICIAL_SECONDARY
Authority note: This note is not the Boston Zoning Code and is not BPDA or ISD guidance.

Residents seeking parcel-specific zoning, permitted uses, or zoning relief should consult:
- Inspectional Services Department (ISD) for permits, occupancy, and zoning enforcement questions
- Boston Planning Department / BPDA materials published on boston.gov for district maps and adopted plans
- The official Boston Zoning Code as published by the City of Boston

On the Porch may summarize curated secondary references, but answers must not present this note as adopted city law or parcel determination.
