Title: Boston Zoning and Inspectional Services — resident reference overview
Publisher: City of Boston / Inspectional Services Department (ISD)
Official URL: https://www.boston.gov/departments/inspectional-services
Geography: Boston / Dorchester
Publication context: Public city guidance for residents seeking zoning and building-permit information.

Boston's Inspectional Services Department (ISD) administers building permits, occupancy,
and many zoning enforcement questions for property owners and residents. Zoning districts
and allowed uses are defined in the Boston Zoning Code and related BPDA planning documents.
Accessory dwelling units, home occupations, and neighborhood-specific overlay districts may
apply in Dorchester; residents should confirm parcel-level rules with ISD or the Boston
Planning Department rather than relying on secondary summaries alone.

Source limitations: This overview is a curated public-reference digest for On the Porch
development and staging. It is not a legal determination for any parcel.
