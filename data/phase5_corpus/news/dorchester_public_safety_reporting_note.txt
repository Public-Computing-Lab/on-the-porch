Title: How On the Porch treats local news about public safety
Publisher: On the Porch editorial policy note
Official URL: null
Geography: Dorchester
Doc type: news

Local journalism can describe crime trends, community meetings, and city announcements.
On the Porch cites news as JOURNALISM and keeps official BPD / Analyze Boston datasets as
the factual source for counts. News articles should not be treated as live emergency
guidance. Call 911 for immediate danger.
